# Social Affairs

## Etiquette of intermingling between opposite genders

### Relationship between males and females

\QUESTION{123}{2}{5}{Submitted by Zarif from Canada on Mar 31, 2003}

_As-Salāmu ʿalaykum_. If I forget to pass wet hands over my socks while making
_wuḍūʾ_ and then I sit down and remember, can I go back and just pass the wet
hands over my socks or do I have to remake my whole _wuḍūʾ_? Also, can you go
into much more detail as to what seclusion (_khalwah_) is, so that there is no
ambiguity amongst the people?

Answer

_Wa ʿalaykum as-Salām wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh_.

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

As for your forgetting to pass wet hands over your socks, you just need to pass
the wet hands over them when you remember. You need not remake your whole _wuḍūʾ_.

As for the meaning of _khalwah_, it refers to a situation in which two unmarried
persons of the opposite sex stay in a closed or isolated place where nobody can
see them easily, and due to this situation, they might commit adultery or
fornication (_zinā_) without being seen by any third party. Islam prohibits
_khalwah_ because it might lead to the prohibited _zinā_ and violation of the
dignity of an innocent girl.

Allah Almighty knows best.



\QUESTION{124}{3}{5}{Submitted by Abdullah on Apr 21, 2003}

Respected scholar, _As-Salāmu ʿalaykum_. What are the limits of the
relationships between males and females? Is it permissible to have boyfriends or
girlfriends? In the first place, is it even permissible to date? What if the
dating occurs in public places (not in secluded areas) and there is no
skin-to-skin contact whatsoever? What if you go out as a group in public areas?
Is it _haram_? Thank you for your time. _Jazākum Allah khayran_.

Answer

_Wa ʿalaykum as-Salām wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh_.

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

Dear brother,
Islam recognises clean friendship and mutual respect among members of the
opposite sex. However, it prohibits any friendship that leads to dating or
seclusion with a member of the opposite sex (_khalwah_). Taking into account the
nature of the concept of girlfriend or boyfriend, it is clear that Islam does
not recognise such a friendship.

However, if two members of the opposite gender are interested in getting
married, they have to make it known through their parents and avoid any contact
without the presence of an unmarriageable male (_maḥram_). This is due to the fact
that Satan may entice them to fall into _haram_.

Of course, the prohibition of dating is not limited to closed places only; it
covers all places, whether closed or open, as long as there is dating between
two members of the opposite gender. Furthermore, it does not matter if there is
skin contact or not. One should try to do his or her best to avoid the
occurrence of this issue. Parents have to work on this and help their daughters
and sons to uphold Islamic principles in dealing and interacting with the
opposite gender. May Allah guide all of us.

Allah Almighty knows best.

\QUESTION{141}{20}{5}{Submitted by Saeed from Pakistan on Aug 25, 2003}

If the Qurʾān can be touched only by the pure, how can a non-Muslim get the
benefit from the Qurʾān without touching it?

\ANSWER

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

In my opinion, there is no definitive evidence in the Qurʾān or authentic Sunnah
categorically prohibiting non-Muslims or Muslims without ablution (_wuḍūʾ_) from
touching the Qurʾān. The evidence cited by some scholars is open to differing
interpretations, while the authenticity and implications of the relevant _hadiths_
remain subjects of scholarly debate.

The verse “None touch it except the purified” (Al-Wāqiʿah, 56:79) does not, in
my understanding, refer to non-Muslims or those without ablution touching the
physical Qurʾān. Rather, it concerns the protection of divine revelation, as
supported by the verses: “And the devils have not brought it down. Nor is it
appropriate for them, nor are they able to do so” (Al-Shuʿarāʾ, 26:210--211).
Accordingly, I see no harm in allowing non-Muslims or Muslims without ablution
to touch and read the Qurʾān, particularly when doing so facilitates learning,
reflection, and understanding of its divine guidance. Allah Almighty knows best.

### Care of animals

\QUESTION{164}{43}{5}{Submitted by Yolla on Dec 14, 2004}

_As-Salām ʿalaykum_. Is it _haram_ (prohibited) to de-claw a young cat to
protect the furniture? We adopted a cat a year ago and tried to train it not to
scratch. However, it has ruined some of our furniture, and we do not want to
send it back to the shelter, for the kids enjoy having it around. _Jazākum Allah
khayran_.

\ANSWER

Islam calls upon Muslims to fear Allah in everything including animals. Islam
also calls upon Muslims to be gentle and nice to everything and at all times,
even when they want to slaughter animals.

In this context, de-clawing a cat is a form of harming, oppressing, and
depriving it from its rights of having claws, which it needs--not to scratch your
furniture but to scratch to defend itself.

Therefore, you should let your cat grow its claws and find other ways of
protecting your furniture other than hurting or harming innocent and friendly
animals like cats.
