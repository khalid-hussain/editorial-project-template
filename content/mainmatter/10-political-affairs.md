means and examine its impact and contribution towards putting a quick end to the
atrocities against the Palestinians.

not be targeted. I mean by that children, women, and old people who are not
aggressors or oppressors. Furthermore, properties and the lives of animals have

innocent people.

All in all, I call upon the beloved Palestinian scholars and policy makers to
re-evaluate and review from time to time all methods and means, especially
_istishhādī_ operations, peace processes, etc. that they have been using in
their legitimate struggle against oppression and injustice. I believe there is
no need to import _fatwas_ about this means from those who are not living under

scholars outside Palestine should trust the Palestinian scholars living under
occupation to deliver the correct _fatwa_ related to this issue. Let us pray to
Allah to punish oppressors and aggressors, and let us pray to Him to protect the
lives of children, old people, women and all innocents.

against Palestinians. Therefore, let us agree that those who oppose the policy
of Zionists should not be considered as soldiers and oppressors to Palestinians.

same thing applies to children, women and old people.

Allah Almighty knows best.
