# Sports and Recreation

## How youths can spend their summer

### Summer camps

\QUESTION{185}{1}{8}{Submitted by Kamal on Jun 7, 2004}

How do Muslim youth camps help in bringing Muslim families and individuals of
diverse backgrounds together?

\ANSWER

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

Surely, a Muslim youth camp is one of the ways of bringing Muslims from
different backgrounds together.

In this regard, the camp should be designed with a mission of creating a
platform for Muslim families and individuals to meet one another and look for
ways and means of cooperation and coordination in the future for mutual benefit
and interest.

Islam always stands for strong cooperation and ties among Muslims. Muslim youth
nowadays should transcend all traditional barriers and obstacles of the past.
They should live their time based on cooperation and communication among
nations, aiming to participate positively in the development of the world and
mankind at large.

Accordingly, there should be strong commitment towards fostering unity,
cooperation, solidarity, and communication among Muslim families and
individuals. To achieve this noble purpose, the camp program should be well
planned and comprehensive. Having said all this, there is no harm in spending
some time for entertainment and relaxing.

Muslim families and individuals are strongly recommended to relax during the
holidays and vacations. This is in line with the Prophetic statement that says:
“Your body has a right upon you, as your Lord has a right. Therefore, give each
of them their right.” Thus, Muslims need to relax and take vacation for leisure.

Allah Almighty knows best.





\QUESTION{186}{2}{8}{Submitted by Falih on Jun 7, 2004}

Would you please give advice to Muslim parents to reward their children with a
summer camp in which they can have a good time together? What do you think of
this?

\ANSWER

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

Thank you. It is strongly recommended in Islam that the parents should spend
some of their time with their beloved children especially during the summer.
This would help the parents to exchange views and ideas with their children
about different issues and matters of their concern. It is a good idea to reward
children with a comprehensive and constructive summer camp.

In this regard, parents should take a friendly part in planning for summer camp
for their children. It is an obligation upon them to be concerned about the
contents of the summer camp for their kids in order to protect them from
unwanted items in the camp.

Having said that, the parents should not interfere in all the details of the
camp, meaning their contribution should not be in the form of dictating or
deciding for the kids every single thing. They should give them room to decide
and choose the contents that do not contradict with Islamic teachings. May Allah
grant all parents more wisdom in this matter.

Allah Almighty knows best.


### Youths avoiding temptation

\QUESTION{187}{3}{8}{Submitted by Suad on Jun 7, 2004}

What are some of the ways you advise the youth, especially at universities, to
avoid temptation (_fitnah_). It’s undoubtedly getting harder as summer arrives.

\ANSWER

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

Thank you. As we all know, _fitnah_ is rampant everywhere these days; therefore,
the best way of avoiding it is to get married as soon as possible.

This is based on the Prophetic statement, “O young people! Whoever is able to
get married should do so, and it will be protection for them. But for those who
are not able to do so, they should keep fasting (Mondays and Thursdays); and
fasting will be protection for them.”

In this regard, I am of the opinion that marriage is a great help for a young
person who wants to avoid _fitnah_. In addition to this, one needs to lower his
or her gaze as required by the Qurʾān in verses 24:30--31. This act is also of
great help in terms of avoiding _fitnah_.

Finally, the youth should remember the Prophetic _duʿāʾ_ which he gave one day
to a young man who couldn’t refrain from adultery. He \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY} prayed
for him by saying: “God help him to be chaste, purify his heart and forgive his
sins.” Accordingly, it is strongly recommended to repeat this _duʿāʾ_ all the
time by saying: “God help me to be chaste, purify my heart and forgive my sins.”

I am sure that the youth should do their best to occupy themselves in doing good
and useful deeds. They should avoid having more free time or having too much
interaction with the opposite gender.

Furthermore, they should always have friends who would help them to spend their
time in a proper manner. From deep within my heart, I pray to Allah to protect
the Muslim youth and guide them in their vacation and summer camps. Wish them
all an excellent and fruitful vacation! Allah Almighty knows best.





### Youths spending summer at the beach

\QUESTION{188}{4}{8}{Submitted by Harun from Canada on Jun 7, 2004}

Some brothers suggested the idea of having Islamic beaches for brother and
others for sisters and others for Muslim families so as to protect the
practicing Muslims from watching unveiled women and other scenes, which may be
offensive to their _dīn_ and _ḥayāʾ_. Other brothers said that this will
segregate practicing Muslims and they will not be able to practice _daʿwah_ to
those people. The first group said that this is meant for the protection of our
_dīn_ and _ḥayāʾ_ and it is no use talking to the other people i.e.
non-practicing Muslims because the beach is not a place for _daʿwah_. What do
you think about this issue?

\ANSWER

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

Thank you. It doesn’t seem practical or easy nowadays to have special beaches
for brothers and sisters, especially in non-Muslim countries. Therefore, Muslims
should not spend their efforts and energy in arguing about this issue, at least
for the time-being.

As for _daʿwah_, Islam doesn’t limit it to a specific place or time. However,
one should have strategic plans while making _daʿwah_, meaning, he or she should
evaluate the situation and consequences before undertaking _daʿwah_ work in a
place. Furthermore, one should know the priorities of _daʿwah_.

In this regard, there is no doubt that beaches should not be priority locations
for _daʿwah_ work, especially in their current set up and environment. One
should try to avoid during vacation the beaches that are full of contradiction
with Islamic teachings. Certainly, it would be better not to expose children and
adolescents to these beaches.

Allah Almighty knows best.





### Recreation and tourism in a non-Muslim country

\QUESTION{189}{5}{8}{Submitted by Musa from the United States on May 30, 2005}

I have a question regarding going for vacations in Western countries. Someone
may think of traveling to a Western country for vacation, while someone who
resides in a Western country may think of taking a vacation in his own country.
As you know, summer is here and so is the mood of vacation.

As Muslims, we face the challenge of keeping ourselves upright in the midst of
so much shamelessness and nudity. How can we meet the need of taking the family
out for a few days’ vacation while adhering to the Islamic teachings?

\ANSWER

In this regard, one should travel to any country in the world as long as he or
she can adhere to the teachings of Islam. There is no prohibition of bringing
the family to Western countries. There are many Muslim communities in these
countries; therefore, it is an opportunity to know them and communicate with
them, if possible.

As for the probability of encountering some unpleasant situations in those
countries, one should avoid that at the level of his or her best. I recommend
Muslims in Western countries to visit Muslim countries for better understanding
and in order to look for ways to increase cooperation with fellow Muslims.

Having said all this, one should bear in mind that people are likely to be
influenced in their views about Islam by what they see of Muslim behavior.
Therefore, every Muslim should strive to be a good ambassador of Islam and
Muslims wherever they go in this world. One should work towards upholding the
good image and name of Islam. May Allah guide all of us and protect Islam and
Muslims.

Allah Almighty knows best.





### _Daʿwah_ activities

\QUESTION{190}{6}{8}{Submitted by Funsho on Jun 15, 2003}

Dear Sheikh, nice to see you on IslamOnline. Concerning summer activities, can
you please explain to us some _daʿwah_ activities that we Muslim youth in the
West should focus on? And what are the things that we need to do to preserve our
_īmān_ during this period of year when youth are more prone to be irresponsible?
Thanks.

\ANSWER

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

Thanks, brother Funsho. As for _daʿwah_ activities I recommend that you, as well
as other Muslim youth in the West, keep in mind the following:

1.  Be good ambassadors of Islam wherever you go. As an ambassador, you are
    expected to introduce the right and wonderful teachings of your religion to
    others. As an ambassador, you are expected to correct the wrong and false
    image and feelings of hatred towards Islam.

2.  Fear your Lord, Allah, wherever you are. The Prophet \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY} is
    reported to have said: “Fear Allah wherever you are; follow a bad deed with
    a good one; and behave in a good manner.”
  
    Indeed, fearing Allah means to observe Islamic teachings by doing all the
    obligatory acts, and avoiding all the prohibited ones. Vacation times are
    supposed to add more and more benefit to the life of the Muslim. In holiday
    times, religious awareness should be increased.

3.  Be keen to recite the Qurʾān from time to time and enhance your good
    relationship with it. Try to memorise a few extra verses and get to know new
    Prophetic hadiths. The Prophet \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY} used to pray to Allah to
    make the Qurʾān like a spring for his heart and to make it the source for
    solutions to his problems and difficulties. Try to do the same thing, so
    specify some of your time for reading and learning its meanings.

Try to accompany those who are righteous and honest all the time. Being alone
will give Satan a chance to push you to commit evil activities. Therefore, you
should try your best to be with righteous people who always remind you and
protect you from Satan and his partisans.

Finally, I stress that the image of Islam in the West needs to be corrected
peacefully and quickly. You should take part in this noble and urgent mission by
disassociating our beloved religion from all evils and violations. Various
communities could be your targets for correcting the wrong image. May Allah
bless your efforts.

Allah Almighty knows best.

the youth these days?

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

Thanks for this important question. Surely, the youth nowadays need a lot of
things. This is due to the challenges and opportunities of this century. I
deeply believe that the most important and urgent need is education. Muslim
youths have no choice but to become educated. They have to excel at least in one

excellent in any branch of knowledge they choose as an area of specialisation.

In addition to education, I call upon them to get to know each other and to
increase their understanding of the situations of Muslims throughout the world.
They have to cooperate and communicate among themselves. They have to close all
areas of difference for the sake of unity and cooperation. They should bear in
mind that there will be no change in Muslims’ situation today if they don’t work
hard and cooperate among themselves.

Islam stands for mutual love and respect among its followers. Muslim youth have
to change all wrong images and misconceptions of Islam in the media. They have
to correct the image of violence and barbarism. They have to demonstrate the
peaceful and cooperative face of their great religion which condemns all types
of violence and killing of innocents and helpless people.

All in all, the youth are the hope of Islam and Muslims. Let history witness and
record their determination to change the backwardness and miserable situation in
which Muslims are living nowadays. I am full of hope that with educated and
skillful youth, Islam will prevail one day, _inshāʾ Allah_.

As for what the youth should do during the summer, they should seek more
knowledge in any important fields related to or close to their specialisation.

Allah Almighty knows best.


## Should girls be allowed to learn to swim?

\QUESTION{192}{8}{8}{Submitted by Maryam from the United States on Jun 7, 2004}

I read a _hadith_ that every man, woman and child must learn to swim, ride
horses, and do archery. I assume this _hadith_ includes girls. How can a girl
learn to swim if she cannot wear a bathing suit? Is it _halal_ for her to wear a
swimsuit so long as only women are around?

My husband thinks even if there are only women you can never be sure there are
no cameras or something, so you shouldn’t wear a bathing suit, period. What
should I do about my seven-month-old daughter?

\ANSWER

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

Thank you. The _hadith_ you referred to in your question is an authentic one.
All Muslims (including girls) are strongly recommended to learn swimming, riding
horses, and archery.

Certainly, it doesn’t mean that a mature girl should swim or learn to swim in
the presence of any non-_maḥram_ males. It is forbidden to do so. The same thing
applies to a man swimming in the presence of a non-_maḥram_ woman. He has to cover
his _ʿawrah_.

This means that a woman’s or a man’s _ʿawrah_ must be observed while swimming in
the presence of an opposite gender.

As for a woman swimming in the presence of women, she still has to observe the
required _ʿawrah_ while staying with another woman (she has to cover from navel
to the knees only). As for how a girl can learn to swim if she cannot wear a
bathing suit, this is not a big deal with regard to learning. It means she can
wear a bathing suit on the condition that there is no non-_maḥram_ man around.

As for your husband’s fear of cameras being present, this is a very subjective
matter; it depends on the particular location. One should do their best to check
on this matter carefully. However, there is no harm in swimming at night when it
is dark and no man is around. As for your seven-month-old daughter, there is no
prohibition for her to swim with a bathing suit as she is not mature (_bāligh_),
nor is she obliged to cover _ʿawrah_ (except private parts) at the presence of
strangers.

Therefore, you should not be worried about her. Yet, I would stress here that
those three recommended items in the _hadith_ are very crucial at the individual
and communal levels. Horseback riding and archery were essential battle skills
in the past, while today war is conducted with tanks, planes, missiles etc.
Muslims are required to master all these weapons to protect themselves against
attacks.

Allah Almighty knows best.





## Can Muslims play pool, chess, or PC games?

\QUESTION{193}{9}{8}{Submitted by on Jul 5, 2004}

Can I play games like pool, chess, or any other PC game?

\ANSWER

In the Name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
All praise and thanks are due to Allah, and peace and blessings be upon His
Messenger.

Islam stands for the permissibility of games with the following guidelines:

1. The game should be beneficial to the person. It should bring to him that
   which is good not that which is harmful.

2. The games should not hinder the person from observing their religious
   obligations.

3. The games should not lead to any _haram_ or prohibited actions.

With these three conditions, the games should be allowed.

Allah Almighty knows best.
